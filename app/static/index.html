    Pipeline

    How a forecast becomes a dispatch decision

    1. 01 · CLEAN

      Telemetry in

      Hourly temperature, humidity and solar irradiance from the sample set, your CSV or typed values. Timestamps are parsed, duplicates dropped, gaps interpolated and out-of-range readings clipped.

    2. 02 · PREDICT

      QuickML regression

      A linear model on hour_0…hour_23 plus cooling_load = max(0, temp − 24 °C), published as a Catalyst QuickML endpoint. If the endpoint is unreachable, the page says so and shows an offline estimate.

    3. 03 · DISPATCH

      Rules decide each hour

      Every forecast hour is checked against the thresholds below. Overload alerts are raised independently of the grid mode.

    Dispatch rules
    ConditionResult
    demand > 160 kWRenewable Storage
    hour 18:00–22:00 (inclusive)Renewable Storage
    otherwiseMain Grid
    demand ≥ 200 kWCritical overload alert in either mode
